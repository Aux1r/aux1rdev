## Who am I?

I'm Kacper (aux1r being my GitHub username), a university student currently studying a **BEng in Software Engineering**. I have a lot
of interests including cybersecurity, low-level and embedded programming,
operating systems. Outside of tech, I really enjoy cooking and baking as well as travel - I love getting to know people from other countries and learning about their languages and cultures (I would spend the rest of my life travelling the world if I could).

Whilst still at university, I was the **President** of [**CompSoc**](https://comp-soc.com/), Scotland's largest tech society in 2025-26 and was a key organiser in [**HackTheBurgh XI and XII**](https://hacktheburgh.com/). I love to get speak with other CS students, hobbyists and professionals, so feel free to reach out if you wanna talk about anything!

## What is this site?

This site is basically just a blog where I write about anything I find interesting, the stuff I get up to and any projects I wanna showcase or write a little more than just a github README.md about. I'm not some viral tech blogger or awesome coding genius so don't expect miracles in these blog posts, but I think they'll be a nice way to show off my work and get my ideas and opinions out there about computers, software and everything else tech-related.
